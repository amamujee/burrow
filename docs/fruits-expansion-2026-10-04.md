# Exotic fruit expansion — 4 October 2026

The catalog grows from 125 to 150 fruit cards. This addition preserves all previous records and photographs and adds 25 distinct regional fruits across six inhabited continents. These new species are distinct from the pre-existing catalog; the full catalog still includes some cultivar and market groups.

All new cards use the Hard discovery pool and the existing game modes. Flavor and availability remain editorial ratings, not laboratory measurements or global inventories. Origins retain regional scope and map pins represent one place within that scope.

## Measurements

All 25 additions have a sourced size example. Ice Cream Bean compares the length of its whole pod (60 cm), becoming the longest example in the collection; this does not mean it weighs more than jackfruit. The 25 sizes describe whole fruit bodies or pods, excluding stems and projecting bristles. Marang, camu camu, uvaia and arazá add four scoped whole-fruit weights, bringing documented weights to 69 and unknown weights to 81. Large cultivated arazá is explicitly distinguished from small wild fruits.

Per-card review dates keep the original records' review dates intact. The table links measurement references; each source record retains additional references for identity, flavor and origin.

## Rebuilding and validation

Run `node scripts/fingerprint-fruit-images.mjs`, `node scripts/generate-fruits-pack.mjs` and `npm run generate:offline-manifest` after source or photo edits. Regeneration is offline.

The fruit regressions cover the 150-card count, all 25 addition IDs, six-continent coverage, distinct local photo hashes and source pages, image dimensions, attribution, scoped sizes and weights, complete collection ordering and reachability. The tablet and mobile flow opens Uvaia, checks its size and weight, and exercises photo enlargement, zoom, credit and recovery.

Validated on 4 October 2026 with `BURROW_PLAYWRIGHT_PORT=3218 npm run verify`: lint, pack validation, image and offline-manifest checks, content QA, 128 logic tests, the production build, 66 desktop checks and 34 mobile checks passed. Five desktop cases were skipped by their existing device guards. The new Uvaia card and enlarged-photo screenshots were also reviewed visually. The previous 125 source records, generated cards and photo bytes were compared with the base revision and remain unchanged.
