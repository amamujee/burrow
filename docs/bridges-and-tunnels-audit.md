# Bridges and tunnels audit — 5 September 2026

The collection contains 62 crossings: 38 in the United States and 24 elsewhere. The 42 existing card IDs remain; 20 US crossings were added.

Every card has a reference for its length and opening year, a representative crossing point or tunnel portal, and a photo source with creator and license attribution. US cards also name their state or states. Fame and Engineering remain subjective gameplay ratings.

Lengths describe the named structure or crossing. Notes distinguish full crossings, approach roads, branches, bridge-only measurements, and individual parallel bores. Opening notes distinguish original structures, replacements, and later spans. These scopes matter when comparing the numbers.

## US map

The state outlines are the US Census Bureau’s 2017 cartographic boundaries, distributed as [us-atlas 3.0.1](https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas). All 50 states and Washington, DC are present. Alaska and Hawaii use labeled insets at different scales. A shared projection layout places state paths and crossing pins in the same coordinate system. State labels use the main polygon’s area centroid, with adjustments for irregular shapes and callouts for small eastern states.

Geo Finder uses four US crossing choices for US cards when enough separated candidates are available, and retains world rounds for international cards. Switching views preserves pin letters and choices. Selecting a state is an exploration action; only a lettered pin or answer choice submits a game answer.

Regenerate the catalog with `node scripts/generate-bridges-and-tunnels-pack.mjs`; regenerate boundaries with `node scripts/generate-us-map.mjs`; update offline assets with `npm run generate:offline-manifest`.
