# Tall Trees content audit — 5 September 2026

## Scope and learning contract

Tall Trees contains 31 cards: 27 real tree subjects and the four existing human, giraffe, shuttle and tower height references. The expansion adds 12 distinct species; it does not add screens, dependencies or gameplay rules. Children compare height, distinguish kinds of tree and learn concrete identification and ecology facts. Familiar species balance more obscure forest giants. Difficulty is 12 Easy, 11 Medium and 8 Hard, based on recognition rather than height.

All 19 original card IDs remain stable. Three misleading individual cards now describe the species shown by their existing photos: Douglas Fir, Sitka Spruce and Yellow Meranti. Coast Redwood replaces the old chart-based Hyperion presentation; Hyperion remains the sourced height example and appears in its fact. General Sherman, Centurion and Doerner remain named individual trees.

## Repairs made

- Added factual sources and an explicit accuracy/measurement note to every card, separate from image provenance. The notes distinguish named individuals, exceptional species examples, ordinary mature ranges and historical heights.
- Corrected General Sherman to 83.8 m, Centurion to the 96.5 m entry in the 2025 field-study table, southern blue gum to 90.7 m, and the giant-sequoia exceptional reference to 96.3 m.
- Removed the unsupported 91.3 m living White Knight claim. The 2025 study says the previously listed tall manna gum was dead by 2020; Manna Gum now uses the Victorian flora's stated 50 m height example. Noble Fir uses the US Forest Service's 70 m mature comparison, without an unsourced current record claim.
- Preserved Menara's valid 97.6 m comparison: the 2019 study gives 98.90 m and 96.26 m from opposite bole-base points, whose average is 97.58 m. Its widely quoted 100.8 m starts at the lowest buttress. The species card explains both methods.
- Kept the 100.5 m Mountain Ash value explicitly historical, distinct from the later measurement on Centurion's individual card.
- Corrected Karri from an “eucalyptus relative” to a eucalyptus, and removed the accidental combination of an Australian image with a Portuguese record-tree claim.
- Repaired geographic descriptions to identify native ranges or named native-range examples, and distinguished planted-photo locations from native origin. Geography stays absent for fictional Dave and Geoffrey, so they cannot enter Geo Finder.
- Corrected Big Ben's fact: the compared structure is Elizabeth Tower, while Big Ben is the bell. Shuttle height is the complete upright stack, not the orbiter by itself.
- Recalculated every stack from the displayed feet: divide by 6 for Dave or by 18 for Geoffrey, then round consistently. The human's giraffe fraction remains a tenth-based value.
- Replaced the inaccurate Doerner illustration with a verified photograph of that tree. Replaced Karri's name-sign photo and Hyperion's multi-subject answer-labeled chart with real species photographs. Original image files remain on disk; no existing source image was overwritten.
- Repaired alt text against actual images: the English oak and new sugar maple show leaves; the kapok image shows its crown; the mountain ash image shows roadside forest. Credits now name the actual photographer and reuse license.
- Standardized meaningful categories such as conifer, flowering tree, eucalyptus, evergreen and deciduous. Ginkgo is correctly excluded from conifers; dawn redwood is correctly deciduous.
- Removed Top Trumps only from this pack's recommendations: feet, meters, Dave stacks and giraffe stacks all measure the same property, so they cannot provide independent strategic choices. Sort, Fact, Peek, Numbers, Odd One and Geo Finder remain recommended.

## New content and images

The 12 new subjects are Western Redcedar, Ponderosa Pine, Eastern White Pine, Tulip Tree, Sugar Maple, American Sycamore, Ginkgo, Dawn Redwood, Monkey Puzzle, Kapok, African Baobab and River Red Gum. They add useful variation from about 80 feet to the existing forest giants, with intentionally honest ties. Botanical heights are not claimed to be exact measurements of the photograph.

All 15 new local image files (12 additions plus three repairs) are real openly licensed photographs, with exact original filenames, authors, licenses and source pages recorded below. Wikimedia's existing thumbnails were used where its original-file service rate-limited requests. Images were resized/re-encoded only; subjects were not generated or altered. New image payload is approximately 3.74 MB before the app's existing image optimization.

Visual QA inspected the existing and new cards as contact sheets, then inspected the three replacements and sharper white pine image. The photos match the named species or individuals and are visibly distinct. The two existing GFDL images retain their authors and full license URLs in credits; new acquisitions use CC BY, CC BY-SA or public-domain licenses.
